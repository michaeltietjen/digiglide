How it plays

DigiGlide is switched on per track. It works on FM TONE, WAVETONE, FM DRUM and SWARMER tracks, with whatever sound is on them. With it off, a track is exactly stock.

01

One key, one chord

The note you play is the root. Set a key and each root plays the chord that belongs to it, or choose the type and the added notes yourself: major, minor, sus, dim or open fifths, with 6, 7, maj7 and 9.

02

Voices glide

When the next chord comes, each voice slides to its new note. Set how long it takes, from a sixteenth note to sixteen bars or a full minute, and the curve it follows.

03

Hold and shape

With HOLD on, a chord stays after you let go, so the next one always glides from it. Turn VOIC and POS under a held chord and it reshapes while it rings.

The sequencer writes the progression: trigs carry the roots, and chord type, voicing, glide time and more can be locked per step.

More in it

Ways to strike a chord

PLAY chooses all notes at once, a strum up, a strum down, or a harp run up through three octaves.

  • CHRD
  • STRM
  • STRU
  • HARP

Moves

A gesture for how the voices travel into a chord. Good as a lock on a single step.

DigiDrone sounds

Ten FM TONE drone sounds are built in, stepped through and loaded with two shortcuts. Each note of the chord is its own small network of oscillators.

  • STILL AIR
  • SLOW CHAIN
  • DARK RING
  • IRON BELLS
  • BEAT FIELD
  • GLASS ORGAN
  • UNDERTOW
  • WIRE CHOIR
  • HOLLOW FIFTHS
  • STORM GLASS

With the stock arpeggiator

Turn the Digitone's own ARP on and it plays the chord of the key you hold, one note at a time, with its usual speed, range and note length.

Two tracks can sound chords at once, for example a drone on one and glides on another.

Where it stands

DigiGlide is being built and played on one unit, the author's. This page describes image 35. Features and storage change between builds, and there is no installer and no download yet.

Known limits

  • Tested on one unit. Most checks run in an emulator, not on hardware.
  • A heavy pattern can still run out of time when two DigiGlide tracks play many voices, and other tracks then lose notes.
  • A pattern change carries the settings only to a track holding the same sound.
  • Kit copy, SAVE KIT and exports drop the settings.
  • A rare glitch noise after about two minutes of play has been reported and is not explained yet.
  • It does not work on MIDI tracks.

The voice budget

The Digitone II has 16 voices, and a DigiGlide track reserves its own. A budget that works: one glide track with 6 voices, one drone with 4, and 6 left for drums and bass.

Testing the beta

When it is ready

It will be free: the source, plus a way to build it from your own copy of the official OS file. No Elektron firmware will be shared.

Before you install any modified firmware

DigiGlide is unofficial. It is not made, endorsed or supported by Elektron. Installing modified firmware is at your own risk: it can leave the unit unable to start, and it may affect your warranty.

Back up the +Drive, keep the official OS file at hand, and have a DIN MIDI interface ready, not only USB. If the unit starts but misbehaves, send the official OS file with Elektron Transfer over USB. If it does not start, hold [FUNC] while powering on, press [TRIG 4], and send the official OS file to the MIDI IN socket. That takes about 12 to 15 minutes.

The repository holds no Elektron firmware, nothing extracted from it and no file generated from it. A DigiGlide image is built on your own computer from your own copy of the official OS 1.12 file.

Where it comes from

DigiGlide is a port of Brickglide, an ambient chord machine for handhelds, and it stands on other people's work on the Digitone II:

  • dnfw by angellinares: the firmware container, loader, parameter table relocation, safety check and USB probe.
  • digihealth and digiemu by irpina: the origin of the probe, and the emulator.
  • digikit by m-dwyer: the emulator tooling the tests run in.
  • digitone-2-modding-skill by complex-ping: build helpers and the emulator harness.
  • dn2-cfw (Digiwerk) by naturarum: notes on the kit record and the parameter table.
