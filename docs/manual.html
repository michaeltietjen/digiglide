The idea

A synth track with DigiGlide on plays a whole chord from one key or one trig. When the next chord comes, the voices glide into it instead of jumping. The note you play is the chord's root; TYPE, EXT and KEY decide its notes.

It works on FM TONE, WAVETONE, FM DRUM and SWARMER tracks. It does not work on MIDI tracks.

Switching it on

  1. Select a synth track.
  2. Press [SYN] until the DigiGlide pages appear. There are four, after the machine's own pages: CHORD, MOTION, SETUP, TUNE.
  3. On SETUP, turn knob A (VCNT) from OFF to 3, 4, 5 or 6. That is the switch, and the number of voices the chord uses, the bass voice included.

Shortcut: [FUNC] + push knob E flips the current track between OFF and its last voice count. The screen shows "DIGIGLIDE ON (5 VOICES)" or "DIGIGLIDE OFF".

With VCNT OFF a track is exactly stock.

Voices

The Digitone II has 16 voices. A DigiGlide track reserves its voices for itself (the stock VOICE menu's VOICES setting; DigiGlide raises it for you and shows "VOICES SET TO 5"). Voices reserved by one track are not available to the others.

  • If the screen shows "VOICES 3 OF 5", other tracks hold the rest. Lower their VOICES in the VOICE menu.
  • If it shows "OTHER TRACKS: NO VOICES", every voice is reserved and tracks without a reservation are silent.
  • Switching VCNT OFF hands the voices back.

A budget that works: one glide track with 6 voices, one drone with 4, and 6 left for drums and bass.

Two DigiGlide tracks at once

Two tracks can sound chords at the same time, for example a drone on one and glides on another. A third DigiGlide track that plays while both are sounding gets a single stock note. A track with HOLD on keeps its place until its chord is let go.

With four voices, MAJ with EXT 9 or 6+9 plays root, fifth and ninth with no third. If a chord sounds hollow, try EXT MAJ7, 7 or 6, or use five voices.

HOLD: a held chord ends when you play the same chord again by hand, press [STOP], mute the track, or turn HOLD off. A sequenced trig that repeats the chord strikes it again instead.

P-locks

Hold a trig and turn the knob, as for any stock parameter. These can be locked per step: TYPE, EXT, POS, VOIC, GLID, MOVE, BPAT, BLOM, TUNE, FAN. A lock of VCNT to OFF plays that trig as a normal note.

The stock arpeggiator

With the stock ARP on, it plays the DigiGlide chord of the key you hold, one note at a time, as ordinary stock notes. With ARP MODE CHRD the whole chord is struck on every arp step.

Patterns and kits

DigiGlide's settings are stored with the pattern's kit, per track, and saved with the project.

  • From image 34: when you change to a pattern whose track holds the same sound but has no DigiGlide settings yet, the settings are carried over and the screen shows "DIGIGLIDE KEPT: TRACK n". From then on they belong to that pattern.
  • A track that holds a different sound in the new pattern is left alone. Switch VCNT on there once.
  • SAVE KIT, kit copy and paste, the +Drive kit library and exports do not carry DigiGlide's settings.
  • Loading a sound onto a track keeps the track's DigiGlide settings.

DigiDrone sounds

Ten FM TONE drone sounds are built in. [FUNC] + push knob A steps through them, [FUNC] + push knob B loads the one shown onto the current track. A load replaces the track's sound (save it first if you want it), sets the chord, glide and hold settings that suit it, and switches STRK, BPAT, PLAY and BLOM off. Each wants 4 voices.

STILL AIR, SLOW CHAIN, DARK RING, IRON BELLS, BEAT FIELD, GLASS ORGAN, UNDERTOW, WIRE CHOIR, HOLLOW FIFTHS, STORM GLASS. What each is and what it sets: DIGIDRONE_SOUNDS_29.md.

Play one long trig or one key, then a different root: the chord slides to it. [STOP] lets it go.

If something is wrong

  • Single notes instead of chords: VCNT is OFF on this track in this pattern, the track has fewer than 3 voices reserved, or two other DigiGlide tracks are already sounding.
  • Other tracks lose notes: the unit is out of time. Use fewer DigiGlide voices, or one DigiGlide track instead of two. The status box ([FUNC] + push knob G) shows the time used: a block is 667 microseconds, and H is the longest one seen.
  • Other tracks are silent: all voices are reserved. Lower VOICES on the DigiGlide tracks.
  • A chord that will not stop: [STOP], or turn HOLD off.
  • The unit misbehaves: send the official OS file with Elektron Transfer. See the recovery section of the README.

Known limits

  • Tested on one unit. Most checks run in an emulator, not on hardware.
  • A heavy pattern can still run out of time when two DigiGlide tracks play many voices.
  • A pattern change carries settings only to a track holding the same sound.
  • Kit copy, SAVE KIT and exports drop the settings.
  • A rare glitch noise after about two minutes of play has been reported and is not explained yet.
